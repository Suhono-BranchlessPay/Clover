## Full description

BP Audit Shield permanently anchors every Clover POS payment to the Monad blockchain via BranchlessPay. Tamper-proof records support compliance, tax verification, and customer trust.

Verify any anchored transaction at branchlesspay.com/verify.

Features:
- Automatic payment capture on Clover devices (Clover Android SDK)
- Offline SQLite queue with automatic retry
- Transaction history with Pending / Anchored / Failed badges
- In-app blockchain verification (WebView)
- Share verify URL with customers
- Background foreground service with boot auto-start
- Works on Clover Station, Mini, Flex, Mobile (Android 5.0+)

Configuration: enter your BranchlessPay license key in Settings. No credentials are bundled in the release APK.
