## Full description

BP Audit Shield permanently anchors every Sunmi POS transaction to the Monad blockchain via BranchlessPay. Tamper-proof records support compliance, tax verification, and customer trust.

Verify any anchored transaction at branchlesspay.com/verify.

Features:
- Automatic payment anchoring on Sunmi devices
- Offline SQLite queue with automatic retry
- Transaction history with status badges
- In-app blockchain verification (WebView)
- Share verify URL and print QR on Sunmi printer
- Background service with boot auto-start
- Works on Sunmi T2, V2, P2 series (Android 5.0+)

Configuration: enter your BranchlessPay license key in Settings. No credentials are bundled in the release APK.
